Francine Gentle Cleansing Care is specially formulated for sensitive or irritated skin. Its gentle cleansing action soothes itchiness and discomfort. Its mild alkaline pH and lipid-enriched formula containing burdock respects the skin's natural balance and make it perfect for everyday use.

Designed for intimate confidence and best hygiene

Francine Gentle Cleansing Care is designed for daily care including in multiple situations that develop during feminine life including pregnancy, menopause but also during sports (perspiring, swimming pool, chemicals).

To keep away the common discomfort in your intimate areas (itching, irritation, abnormal odor etc), it is important to adopt the appropriate hygiene practices and use the most appropriate gentle cleansing care.

  • Gentle, soap-free formula
  • Preserves natural pH balance
  • Contains burdock extract with soothing properties
  • Paraben free, coloring agent free
  • Clinically tested by gynecologists and dermatologists

Francine Gentle Cleansing Care can be used like a liquid soap. Make it foam then rinse well.
