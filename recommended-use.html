Recommended Uses

Designed for Intimate hygiene: soothes and protects sensitive or irritated skin

Francine Gentle Cleansing Care is designed for intimate hygiene: soothes and protects sensitive or irritated skin.

Francine Gentle Cleansing Care is specially formulated for sensitive or irritated skin. Its gentle cleansing action soothes itchiness and discomfort. Its mild alkaline pH and lipid-enriched formula containing burdock respects the skin's natural balance and make it perfect for everyday use.

Tolerance

Soap, paraben, coloring agent, artificial perfume and phenoxyethanol free. Clinically tested by gynecologists and dermatologists.

Recommended use

Use Francine Gentle Cleansing Care as a liquid soap. Make it foam then rinse well. Gentle enough to be used once or twice a day, Francine Gentle Cleansing Care is recommended for sensitive skin or in case of intimate discomforts (including irritation, itching). For external use only.

Indications

Best hygiene in multiple situations that develop during feminine life including pregnancy, menopause but also during sports (perspiring, swimming pool chemicals)
